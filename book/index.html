About the book

What happens when a smart soccer ball learns from all the WRONG examples?

Ten-year-old Nolan is PUMPED when his coach gives him a high-tech AI soccer ball to test. There's just one problem: the SmartBall 3000 tells him to close his eyes before shooting!

With help from his younger brother Nathan and their tech-savvy dad, Nolan discovers the AI learned from terrible examples, the kindergarten team's "silly soccer" game! Can Nolan reset the AI and teach it the right way? And why does their dog Oreo keep trying to steal the ball?

What kids will learn

  • How AI learns from examples and patterns
  • Why the quality of training data matters
  • Critical thinking about technology

Includes discussion questions, AI words to know, and a try-it-at-home activity. A perfect read-aloud for classrooms, libraries, and STEM programs.
