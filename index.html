Alternative Data Tool

The Other Data

Result: Visitors share it, and it's already drawing people in from Facebook and Instagram.

A free tool that explains stocks with alternative data — the offbeat, indirect signals most people miss. Look up a ticker and get plain-English AI summaries of news and quotes, plus offbeat public data like paperboard output, airport lines, and FDA reports. Education only, not predictions. Built for insight, not candlestick-chart reading.

How I built it

Problem: Most stock tools assume you can read charts. I wanted plain-English explanations of the offbeat signals behind a stock.

Approach: I pulled market data from the Finnhub and Tiingo APIs and used Gemini to turn news and quotes into short summaries. I used AI assistants to plan and build it, and tested the output by hand.

Key decision: I left out candlestick charts on purpose and built the page around insight instead.

AI Tool

Does AI Know My Business? (AI Visibility Check)

Result: 8+ AI visibility checks completed, and it has already captured its first lead.

A free tool that shows whether AI recommends a local business. An AI agent runs live Google searches on screen, then gives a 0–100 score, the competitors AI names instead, a check of the business's website, and a fix plan with ready-to-paste schema code. It works in English and Spanish. Built with Gemini + Google Search grounding, Vercel, and JavaScript.

How I built it

Problem: Local business owners don't know whether AI assistants recommend them, or why a competitor gets named instead.

Approach: An AI agent runs live Google searches with Gemini's Search grounding, then scores the result 0–100. I tested and refined the prompts so scores stay consistent and explainable.

Key decision: The searches play live on screen so owners see how the score was reached, and every result comes with a fix plan and ready-to-paste schema code. It works in English and Spanish.

Kids' Learning App

How AI Works for Kids

Result: 14+ lessons finished by kids so far.

Interactive lessons where kids ages 6–10 learn how AI works by teaching a tiny AI named Pip. There are 15 lessons in two sections, and each one has a picture story with read-aloud, a hands-on game (including a real nearest-neighbor classifier that learns a kid's secret sorting rule, and a next-word model that co-writes a story), a quick check, and a badge, plus a printable certificate at the end. Approach: Fifteen lessons, each with a read-aloud story and a hands-on game, including a real nearest-neighbor classifier and a next-word model. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind and Vercel, with AI assistants for planning and building.

Key decision: It's kid-safe by design: no ads, no accounts, no chatbot and no kid data. I tested it with my sons.
