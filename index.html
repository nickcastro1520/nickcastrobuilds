AI Tool

Does AI Know My Business? (AI Visibility Check)

A free tool that shows whether AI recommends a local business. An AI agent runs live Google searches on screen, then gives a 0–100 score, the competitors AI names instead, a check of the business's website, and a fix plan with ready-to-paste schema code. It works in English and Spanish. I built it with Grok Bot, using Gemini + Google Search grounding, Vercel, and JavaScript.

Kids' Learning App

How AI Works for Kids

Interactive lessons where kids ages 6–10 learn how AI works by teaching a tiny AI named Pip. There are 7 lessons, and each one has a picture story with read-aloud, a hands-on game (including a real nearest-neighbor classifier that learns a kid's secret sorting rule, and a next-word model that co-writes a story), a quick check, and a badge, plus a printable certificate at the end. It's kid-safe by design: no ads, accounts, chatbot, or kid data (COPPA-friendly). I'm testing it with my sons, Nathan (8) and Nolan (11). I built it with Grok Bot, using Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Vercel, with SEO, schema, GA4, and Search Console.

Alternative Data Tool

The Other Data

A free tool that explains stocks with alternative data — the offbeat, indirect signals most people miss. Look up a ticker and get plain-English AI summaries of news and quotes, plus unusual correlations that can hint at where a stock is headed (think recycling volumes for electronics demand, or shipping-container clues for inventory). Built for insight, not candlestick-chart reading. I built it with Grok Bot.
