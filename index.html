Things I've built

AI Tool

Does AI Know My Business? (AI Visibility Check)

A free tool that shows whether AI recommends a local business. An AI agent runs live Google searches on screen, then gives a 0–100 score, the competitors AI names instead, a check of the business's website, and a fix plan with ready-to-paste schema code. It works in English and Spanish. Built with Gemini + Google Search grounding, Vercel, and JavaScript.

Business Website

A&A Professional Roofers

A full website for A&A Professional Roofers LLC, a Chicago roofing company owned by a friend. It lays out their services (repairs, replacements, inspections, storm damage, flat roofs), shows a gallery of their work and the suburbs they serve, and puts a "Get a Free Quote" button and a tap-to-call phone number at the top of the page. I built it with Claude and Replit, and gave it a Spanish-speaking callout so the owners reach more of their customers.

About me

So far I've built a free tool that checks whether AI recommends a local business, a business website for a local roofing company, a free game-idea tool for Roblox developers, and a children's book that explains machine learning. I also produce AI Tools Pulse, a short daily podcast about new AI tools. I'm looking for my next project, whether that's a role on a team or a website for your business.
